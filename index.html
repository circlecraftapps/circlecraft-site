FROM THEORY TO MUSIC

Understand why chords work together

Instead of treating the Circle of Fifths as a static diagram, CircleCraft connects keys, diatonic chords, Roman numerals, harmonic function and progressions in one visual workflow.

01

Explore harmony visually

Select a key and see its diatonic chords and harmonic relationships directly on the Circle of Fifths.

02

Build progressions

Tap chords to construct progressions, transpose them while preserving Roman-numeral relationships, save them and play them back.

03

Hear your ideas

Turn theory into sound with guitar-based chord playback, tempo control and looping.

04

Progression intelligence

Analyse harmonic movement, cadence behaviour, chord tones and voice movement, then explore suggested next chords.

05

Learn and practise

Reinforce chord function, Roman numerals and progression knowledge with focused practice and Theory Essentials.

06

Train your ear

Connect the theory you see with the harmony you hear through chord, Roman-numeral, resolution and cadence exercises.
